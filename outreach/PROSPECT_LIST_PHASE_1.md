### Step 2: Personalize for Each Prospect
Replace:
- `[First Name]` → Mike, Sarah, David, Lisa, Tom
- `[Competitor Name]` → Their actual competitor name
- `[Trade]` → HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Cleaning, Pressure Washing
- `[City]` → Phoenix, Denver, Austin, LA, Tampa
- `[trade]` → hvac, plumbing, electrical, cleaning (lowercase for URL)
- `[CALENDAR_LINK]` → Your Calendly link
- `[Your name]` → Your name

### Step 3: Send on Monday 9 AM PT
- Open Gmail / Outlook
- Create 5 new emails
- Paste personalized email body
- Send one every 2-3 minutes
- Log in spreadsheet: Date, Time, Prospect Name, Email Sent

### Step 4: Track Results
- Monitor opens (Tue-Wed)
- Monitor clicks (Wed-Thu)
- Prepare for calls (Thu-Fri)
- Close deals (aim for 35-50% close rate)
