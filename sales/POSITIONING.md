# Positioning — How the Three Tiers Relate

**Stub: Sales positioning matrix for Micro, SMB, Mid-market.**

## The Three Stories We Tell

### MICRO
**Headline:** "Professional website, built in days, not months."

**For whom:** Solopreneurs, just-starting contractors, one-person shops

**Message:**
- "You don't have time for custom builds"
- "You don't want to manage your own website"
- "You need leads, not a project"
- We build it, you launch it, you get customers

**Objection handling:**
- "I can do this myself" → You can, but our design will beat DIY in 2 weeks
- "I want to customize it" → You can edit content; design is fixed but beautiful
- "$49/mo is still something" → Less than coffee a day, captures leads automatically

**Call to action:** "Let's get you live in 4 days"

---

### SMB ← PHASE 1 FOCUS
**Headline:** "Professional branding that competes with bigger players."

**For whom:** Established contractors (3–20 years), multi-service, growing

**Message:**
- "You've built something great; your website should show it"
- "We extract your brand, design around it, launch you professionally"
- "You own your brand; we own the technology"
- You get a site that attracts serious customers, not just calls

**Objection handling:**
- "It's expensive" → $99/mo is cheaper than running ads, captures organic leads
- "We've tried websites before" → Our sites are designed to convert, not just exist
- "What if we want to change it?" → Self-service edits for content, we handle design
- "How fast?" → 5–7 days from intake to live

**Call to action:** "Let's talk about your brand and competition"

---

### MID-MARKET
**Headline:** "Design control that matches your enterprise."

**For whom:** Growing businesses (20+ years, multiple locations, complex service catalog)

**Message:**
- "You don't fit templates"
- "You want full control over your brand and how customers experience it"
- "We build the infrastructure; you design the experience"
- You get a white-label platform that scales with your business

**Objection handling:**
- "This sounds complex" → It is, but our dashboard makes it simple
- "What's the lock-in?" → Own everything; we're just the platform
- "Can you do X?" → Almost certainly; we build what you need
- "How long?" → 10–14 days; it's worth the wait for custom

**Call to action:** "Let's design this together"

## Choosing the Right Tier

**Micro is right when:**
- ☑ Just starting out or solo operator
- ☑ Timeline is critical ("need it next week")
- ☑ Budget is tight (<$60/month)
- ☑ Don't care about customization

**SMB is right when:** (PHASE 1 target)
- ☑ Established business (3+ years)
- ☑ Want professional branding
- ☑ Have services/reviews to show
- ☑ Want to compete with bigger players
- ☑ Willing to pay for quality ($99–500)

**Mid-market is right when:**
- ☑ Enterprise or complex business
- ☑ Multiple locations or service lines
- ☑ Want full design control
- ☑ Have budget for premium ($300+/month)
- ☑ Want white-label solution

## Go-to-Market Strategy

**Phase 1 (Now):** Focus SMB

Reason: Best unit economics, established customer base, proven demand

Messaging: Compete with bigger players, professional branding, recurring revenue

**Phase 2 (Oct):** Add Micro

Reason: High volume, easier upsell path to SMB

Messaging: Fast, simple, cheap to get started

**Phase 3 (Nov+):** Add Mid-market

Reason: High LTV, long sales cycle, enterprise demand

Messaging: Full control, white-label, scale with your business
