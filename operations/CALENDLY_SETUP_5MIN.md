# Calendly Setup — 5 Minutes

**Do this TODAY so your emails have a working link.**

---

## Step-by-Step (5 minutes)

### 1. Go to Calendly (1 min)
- Visit **calendly.com**
- Click "Sign up" (top right)
- Use your email
- Create password
- Done

### 2. Create Event Type (2 min)
- After signup, click "+ Create new event"
- **Event name:** "Sales Call"
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Description:** "Let's talk about your website"
- Click "Next"

### 3. Set Your Availability (2 min)
- Scroll to "Availability"
- **Which days?** Thursday & Friday only (when you'll take calls)
- **Which time?** 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM PT (adjust if different timezone)
- Click "Save"

### 4. Copy Your Link (done)
- Your link is shown: **calendly.com/[your-name]/sales**
- Copy it
- Save it somewhere

---

## That's It

You now have a working calendar link that:
- Prospects can click
- Shows only Thu-Fri availability
- Auto-blocks your time
- Sends you a confirmation email

**Paste this link into your emails** (where it says [CALENDLY_LINK])

---

## If You Want to Customize More

**Brand colors:** Settings → Branding (optional, can skip)
**Time zone:** Settings → Time Zone (make sure it's correct)
**Email notifications:** Settings → Notifications (leave default)

But these are optional. The basic setup above is enough to start.

---

## Testing Your Link

Before you send emails:
1. Copy your calendar link
2. Open it in a new browser tab
3. Check:
   - [ ] Shows "Sales Call" 30 min
   - [ ] Only Thursday-Friday available
   - [ ] Time slot is 2-4 PM (your timezone)
   - [ ] Can book a test meeting

If all look good → your link is ready.

---

## Using Calendly for Calls

When prospect books a time:
1. Calendly sends you email confirmation
2. It appears in your personal calendar
3. At the time, call the prospect using the number from your emails
4. Use `/outreach/SALES-CALL-SCRIPT.md`
5. Close or schedule follow-up

Calendly handles all the scheduling. You just show up and talk.

---

## If You Already Have Calendly

Skip this guide. Just use your existing link in emails. Make sure:
- Availability is set to Thu-Fri 2-4 PM
- Event is called "Sales Call" or something professional
- Time zone is correct
