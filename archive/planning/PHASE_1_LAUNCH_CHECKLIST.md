## Failure Risk Mitigation

**Risk:** Website/brand not ready
**Mitigation:** Landing page is live. Use it as is; polish later.

**Risk:** Videos take too long to record
**Mitigation:** Record rough cuts first; perfect later. Rough > Perfect but missing.

**Risk:** Prospect list incomplete
**Mitigation:** Use what you have; fill in gaps as you go. Don't delay outreach.

**Risk:** Low email open rate
**Mitigation:** A/B test subject lines. If <25%, try Variant B or C.

**Risk:** Low close rate on calls
**Mitigation:** Iterate script. Ask customer for feedback after call.

**Risk:** Can't build first site on time
**Mitigation:** Use a template + basic customization. Ship rough > ship late.
