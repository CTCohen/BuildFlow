### Action 2: Research 1 Real Prospect (45 min)
**Pick ONE trade. Pick ONE city. Research ONE business.**

**Choose your market:**
- [ ] HVAC in Phoenix, AZ
- [ ] Plumbing in Denver, CO
- [ ] Electrical in Austin, TX
- [ ] Cleaning in Los Angeles, CA
- [ ] Pressure Washing in Tampa, FL
- [ ] OR: Your own market choice: _______________

**Research steps (follow exactly):**

1. Open **Google Maps**
2. Search: `[Trade] [City]` (e.g., "HVAC Phoenix")
3. Click on result #4-6 (not #1-3, they're too big to close)
4. Get their info:
   - Business name
   - Owner/manager name (from Google Business profile)
   - Phone number
   - Website (if exists)
   - Email (try: info@[domain], hello@[domain], contact@[domain])

5. Open their website. Note:
   - Is it outdated? (Yes = good prospect)
   - Does it have mobile optimization? (No = good prospect)
   - Does it rank high on Google? (Google their "[Trade] + [City]" term, see if they appear in top 5)

6. Check their competitor:
   - Search same term
   - Note who ranks #1 (that's your competitor angle)

## If You Get Stuck

**"I can't find a prospect's email"**
- Try: info@[domain], hello@[domain], contact@[domain], sales@[domain]
- Check their LinkedIn
- Call them to ask ("Hi, do you have an email I can reach you at?")

**"I don't know who the owner is"**
- Google Business profile shows manager name
- LinkedIn search "[Company Name] [City]" shows employees
- Doesn't matter if you're not 100% sure - "Mike" is close enough

**"I'm not sure if the competitor is right"**
- Search "[Trade] [City]" on Google
- The top 3 results are your competitors
- Pick the one that appears most often

**"The email feels weird"**
- Stop overthinking. Send it.
- The subject line and competitor angle are what work.
- The body is just conversational.
- Authenticity > perfection.
