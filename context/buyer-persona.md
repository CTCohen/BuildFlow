# Buyer personas — BuildFlow

## Primary: local service-business owner

**Who:** Owner-operator or small-team owner of a service business — plumber, HVAC,
electrician, roofer, carpenter, house cleaning, carpet cleaning, pressure washing, junk
removal. Often the person doing the work and answering the phone.

**Web situation:** Either no website, or an old/thin one (Facebook page, a template site
from years ago, a directory listing). Not technical. No time or interest to build one.

**What they care about:** More calls and booked jobs. Looking legit next to competitors.
Not getting ripped off. Not having to learn software.

**What makes them say yes:** A finished, good-looking site handed to them — "here's your
new website, want it?" — with a dead-simple path to make it live. Low or no upfront cost
to start.

**What makes them bounce:** Jargon, long forms, anything that feels like a sales funnel,
monthly fees they don't understand, a bot that clearly isn't a person.

**Language:** English or Spanish. Spanish-first owners are underserved and a real segment.

## Notes

- First 3 are free — the pitch is proof of quality, not price.
- The human (Chase) owns everything from first reply onward.
