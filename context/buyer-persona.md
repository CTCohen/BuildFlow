# Buyer personas: Fornax

## Primary (ICP): SMB trades owner
**Who:** owner or manager of an established trades business (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing), 3-20 employees, $250k-$5M revenue. Already uses or is considering a CRM (HubSpot, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Successware).
**Web situation:** an old or thin site, a template site from years ago, or a directory listing. Not technical; no time to manage a website.
**Cares about:** more booked jobs, looking legitimate next to competitors, leads that land in the CRM without retyping, not learning software.
**Says yes to:** seeing their own finished site in an email, a clear price, going live with one click.
**Bounces on:** jargon, long forms, anything that feels like a sales funnel, fees they don't understand, a bot that clearly isn't a person.

## Secondary: Micro solo contractor
**Who:** solo operator or under 3 employees. Wants a credible site and a place to see leads; has no CRM.
**Says yes to:** low monthly price, no setup, edits by changing text.
**Risk:** higher churn and lower willingness to pay; validate CAC before scaling.

## Not now
Mid-Market (20+ employees, $5M+, multi-location): paused about 12 months.
Spanish-first owners are a real, underserved segment; Spanish output is planned as an opt-in fast follow, not a launch feature.

## Notes
- The tier is assigned by lead scoring, not chosen by the prospect.
- Tyler owns everything from the first human reply onward (high-touch path); the self-serve path needs no human.
