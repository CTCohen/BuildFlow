# Email Subject Lines & Messaging Hooks

**Purpose:** Data-driven email templates for cold outreach to contractor businesses.

Based on competitive research (Agave + 25 competitors), these subject lines and body copy patterns have shown to work.

---

## Principle: Personalization + Problem

Every email must:
1. **Show you researched them** (not a mass template)
2. **Highlight a specific problem** (not "we build websites")
3. **Imply a solution** (not "contact us for a demo")

---

## Subject Line Variants

### Variant A: Curiosity + Authority
**When to use:** Cold outreach to businesses with outdated/weak websites

**Subject Line:**
```
[Name], your competitors are stealing your Google leads
```

**Why it works:**
- Specific competitor mention = research done
- "Stealing" = urgency
- "Google leads" = specific outcome (not vague "web presence")

**Example:**
```
Mike, I noticed [Competitor Name] is ranking higher than you on Google for "[Local Service]"

They're getting calls from searches you should be winning.

Have you looked at your Google Business profile lately? Most contractors miss easy wins there.

[2-min video] → [Your site]

[Your name]
BuildFlow
```

---

### Variant B: Gap Identification + Solution
**When to use:** Outreach to businesses with no website or basic landing pages

**Subject Line:**
```
Quick question: Are you losing calls because of your website?
```

**Why it works:**
- Question format = opens email more
- "Losing calls" = pain point (not abstract benefit)
- Personal tone = not salesy

**Body:**
```
Hi [Name],

I looked at [Business Name]'s current website. Here's what I noticed:

✗ No online quote system (customers can't request quotes easily)
✗ Not mobile-friendly (50% of searches are on phones)
✗ Takes 3 clicks to find your phone number (should be 1)

This means you're losing calls to competitors with better websites.

The fix is simpler than you think. Most contractors can have a fully functional site in 5-7 days and capture leads online.

Interested in a 5-minute conversation? 

[Calendar link to book 15-min call]

[Your name]
BuildFlow
```

---

### Variant C: Social Proof + Offer
**When to use:** Follow-up to non-responders or warm leads

**Subject Line:**
```
[Industry] businesses in [City] are going live this week
```

**Why it works:**
- FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Local specificity
- Trend signal ("businesses... are going live")

**Body:**
```
Hi [Name],

Quick update: [# Contractor Type] in [City] launched new websites last month. 
They're already getting online quotes and calls.

If you want to join them before the holidays/summer rush, we still have availability.

Turnaround time: 5-7 days from start to launch.
Price: $99/mo — we build it, host it, and keep it optimized (cancel anytime)

Want to see an example? [Link to 2 case studies]

[Your name]
BuildFlow
```

## SMS Variants

### Variant A: Direct + Personal
**When to use:** Follow-up to email non-responders (24-72 hours later)

```
Hi [Name]! Quick question: How many leads are you getting from your website?
(Most contractors say "Not many" — that's the problem we solve)

Video: [Short link]
Call? [Calendar link]

– BuildFlow
```

**Why it works:**
- Question format
- Admits they have the problem (relatability)
- Two low-friction CTAs (video or call)

### Variant B: Social Proof + Urgency
**When to use:** After social proof (other contractors in area going live)

```
[Name], 5 [Trade type]s in [City] just went live with new sites.
Getting online quotes + leads already.

Only 3 spots left for this month.

Details: [Short link]

– BuildFlow
```

## Objection Handlers

### Objection: "I don't need a website"
**Response pattern:**
```
Totally get it. Most contractors said the same thing.

Here's what changed: 70% of homeowners search online before calling. 
If you don't show up, they call your competitor.

See the difference: [Link to before/after case study]

[Your name]
BuildFlow
```

### Objection: "I already have a website"
**Response pattern:**
```
Perfect! Many of our customers had one too.

Question: How many online quotes/leads came from it last month?

If it's less than 5, most sites can be rebuilt and kept optimized for $99/mo.

Quick call to see if it's worth it? [Calendar link]

[Your name]
BuildFlow
```

### Objection: "Too expensive / I can't afford it"
**Response pattern:**
```
Understandable. Most of our customers made back the cost in 2-3 new jobs.

Real example: HVAC contractor on the $99/mo plan got 2 online quote requests in the first month.
Each quote → $400-600 job. Covered the year in one month.

Worth 15 minutes to talk about your specific situation? [Calendar link]

[Your name]
BuildFlow
```

## Messaging Themes (to validate with Phase 1 data)

### Theme 1: Speed
- "5-7 days to launch" (vs. weeks with other agencies)
- "Live this week" (urgency + credibility)
- "No waiting" (pain point from competitors)

### Theme 2: Ownership
- "You own the website" (vs. "rent" from other platforms)
- "Full control" (not locked into platform)
- "Your domain, your content" (clarity on what they get)

### Theme 3: Leads
- "Get online quotes" (specific outcome)
- "Capture leads 24/7" (always-on sales)
- "Google finds your customers" (they already search)

### Theme 4: Simplicity
- "$99/mo, cancel anytime" (clarity, no big upfront, no lock-in)
- "We handle hosting/SSL/updates" (one less thing for them)
- "Done in 5-7 days" (not a long project)

## A/B Testing Framework

**Phase 1 hypothesis:**
- Variant A (curiosity) will outperform Variant B (gap) by 15%
- Reason: "Your competitors..." triggers more opens than "Are you losing..."

**Phase 2 test:**
- Subject line variants: Test 3 subject lines on 10 prospects each (30 total)
- Track: Open rate, click rate, reply rate
- Winner: Use for rest of Phase 1

**Phase 3 decision:**
- If A/B test shows winner, commit to it
- If no clear winner, rotate variants

## Personalization Tokens

**Required data per prospect:**
- `[Name]` → First name
- `[Business Name]` → Full legal business name
- `[Industry]` → HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Cleaning, etc.
- `[City]` → Service area city
- `[Competitor Name]` → (optional, for Variant A)
- `[# Contractor Type]` → "5 HVAC contractors", "12 plumbers", etc.

**Data sources:**
- Google Maps search (business name + city)
- LinkedIn (find owner profile)
- Facebook Business page (get category)
- Website (if they have one)

---

## Phase 1 Rollout

**Weeks 1-2:**
- Finalize 3 subject line variants
- Test on first 10 cold outreach prospects
- Track open/click rates

**Weeks 3-4:**
- Analyze results
- Commit to winning variant
- Scale to rest of Phase 1 (5-10 closes target)

**Phase 2:**
- A/B test follow-up sequences (SMS vs. email cadence)
- Test pricing language ("$99/mo" vs. "less than one service call a month" vs. "starting at...")
- Refine objection handlers based on real replies

---

## Success Criteria

✓ Email open rate ≥30% (industry benchmark: 20-25%)
✓ Click rate ≥10% (industry benchmark: 5-7%)
✓ Reply rate ≥15% (industry benchmark: 2-3%)
✓ Call booking rate ≥20% of emails sent (Phase 1 goal)
