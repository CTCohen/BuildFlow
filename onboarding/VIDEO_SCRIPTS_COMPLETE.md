## VIDEO 1: Welcome (1 min)

**[OPEN WITH CONFIDENT SMILE, LOOK AT CAMERA]**

Over the next week, I'm going to show you three things: how to set up your Google Business profile—which is where 60% of your new customers will find you—how to add call tracking so you know which calls come from your site, and how to tell your existing customers about the site.

This takes maybe 20 minutes total, and I've made videos for each step so you can do it at your own pace.

## VIDEO 2: Google Business Setup (3 min)

"Alright, let's set up Google Business so customers can find you.

First: open Google and search your business name. You'll see a box on the right side—that's your Google Business profile. Click 'Claim this business' if you haven't already.

Once inside, go to 'Info.' Make sure everything is correct: your business name exactly as it should appear, your phone number, your address, your hours. Google uses this to rank you when people search for [TRADE] near you.

Next: go to 'Photos.' This is huge. Add 5-10 photos of your work. Before-and-after photos if you have them. Photos of your team. Photos of happy customers. The more photos, the higher you rank.

Third: 'Posts.' Every week, post something. 'New equipment just arrived.' 'Check out this installation.' 'Spring maintenance special.' One post per week. Google's algorithm loves this.

Fourth: 'Reviews.' Ask every customer—after the job is done—to leave a review. Make it easy. Send them a link. Reviews are the #1 ranking factor after photos.

Finally: 'Questions.' Monitor this section. Customers will ask questions. Answer them immediately. This builds trust.

That's it. Do this once, maintain it weekly, and you'll rank higher on Google in 30-60 days. You'll get more calls. That's the whole game.

Next video: call tracking. I'll show you how to see which calls come from your site so you know what's working."

**[END]**

**Time: 180 seconds**
**Key points: Step-by-step. Actionable. Why it matters (ranking). Next video preview.**

## VIDEO 3: Call Tracking Setup (2 min)

"Now let's set up call tracking. This is how you know which calls are coming from your site.

We're using [CallRail/Invoca/your service]. Go to your account. Generate a tracking phone number for your site. It looks like a regular number, but it routes to your real phone.

Put this number on your site. When customers call it from your site, we track it. When they call your regular business line, we don't.

You'll get a dashboard showing: how many calls came from your site, which pages they came from, and how long they talked.

This tells you what's working. If your 'Emergency Service' page is getting 10 calls a week but your 'Maintenance' page gets none, you know what to double down on.

Set this up today. It takes 10 minutes.

Video 4 is about telling your customers about the site. Let's go."

**[END]**

**Time: 120 seconds**
**Key points: Tool setup. Why it matters (ROI tracking). Keep it short.**

---

## VIDEO 4: Tell Your Customers (2 min)

"You've got a new website. Your customers need to know.

Send an email to your customer list: 'Check out our new website. Tell us what you think.' Include the link.

Send a text: 'New website is live. Check it out: [LINK].'

Put a note in every invoice or quote: 'Visit our new site to [schedule service/learn more/etc].'

Tell your team. If customers call, they should mention it: 'Have you seen our new website? That's where you can schedule online.'

That's it. You're not selling. You're just letting people know it exists.

You'll see calls and bookings start coming in from your site within a week or two.

Last video: your monthly checklist. What to do to keep momentum going."

**[END]**

**Time: 120 seconds**
**Key points: Simple distribution. Authentic. Call to action.**

---

## VIDEO 5: Monthly Checklist (1 min)

"Every month, do these five things to keep your website generating leads.

One: post something to Google Business. Doesn't have to be fancy. 'Just finished a big installation.' One photo, two sentences.

Two: ask three customers to leave reviews. Make it easy. Send them the link.

Three: check your call tracking. How many calls came from your site? What pages? Use that to improve next month.

Four: update your photos if you've done new work. Replace old ones with new ones.

Five: answer any questions on your Google Business profile within 24 hours.

That's it. Five things, 15 minutes a month, and your website keeps working.

Any questions? Reply to this email or call me.

Let's get to work."

**[SMILE, END]**

**Time: 60 seconds**
**Key points: Simple, repeatable actions. Low time commitment. Open door for questions.**
