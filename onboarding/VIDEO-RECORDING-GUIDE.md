# Video Recording Guide — Phase 1

Quick setup for recording 5 onboarding videos (rough + authentic).

---

## Equipment (Minimal)

- **Mac:** Built-in camera + ScreenFlow (free trial)
- **Audio:** AirPods Pro or built-in mic (clear enough)
- **Lighting:** Sit facing a window or ring light
- **Background:** Neutral (bookshelf, office okay)

**Total setup time:** 15 minutes

---

## Video 1: Welcome (1 min)

**Setup:**
- Face camera (talking head video)
- Optional: Split screen with your laptop showing the site

**Recording:**
```
"Hey [Customer Name]! Your new website is live.

In the next 7 days, we're going to set up three things:
1. Google Business Profile — so customers find you on Google Maps
2. Call tracking — so you know which leads come from your site
3. Tell your customers about the new site

Each takes 5-10 minutes. Videos are coming in your email.

Your site: [example URL]

Let's get you leads!"
```

**Tips:**
- Smile (warmth matters)
- Speak clearly (not fast)
- One take is fine (authentic > polished)

**Record time:** 5 minutes (including retakes)

---

## Video 2: Google Business Setup (3 min)

**Setup:**
- Screen recording only (your screen, not your face)
- Open Google Business dashboard in Chrome
- Go to google.com/business

**Recording:**
Walk through the steps while narrating:

```
"Let's get you on Google Maps.

Step 1: Go to google.com/business
[Point cursor to "Manage your business" button]

Step 2: Search for [Business Name] in your area
[Type business name in search]

Step 3: If you don't see yourself, click 'Create a business'

Then fill in:
- Business name
- Address
- Phone number
- Website [show where to paste URL]
- Services [pick categories]

Step 4: Google sends a postcard to verify
Takes about 7 days.

Once verified, you show up on Google Maps and Google Search.

That's it! Questions? Email us."
```

**Tips:**
- Slow down (give viewers time to follow)
- Pause at key steps
- Use mouse cursor to highlight (show what to click)

**Record time:** 10-15 minutes (screen recording can be done in one pass)

---

## Video 3: Call Tracking Setup (2 min)

**Setup:**
- Screen recording
- Open CallRail.com
- Open your site admin dashboard

**Recording:**
```
"Let's set up call tracking so you know which calls come from your website.

Step 1: Go to callrail.com
[Show signup page]

Step 2: Enter your phone number

Step 3: Create an account

Step 4: Copy your tracking number
[Show where to find it]

Step 5: Go to your site dashboard
[Go to your site admin]

Settings → Call Tracking → Paste number → Save

Now every call is logged. You'll see:
- Customer name
- Call duration
- Which page they called from

This tells you what's working."
```

**Tips:**
- Show the actual dashboard (make it real)
- Pause at copy/paste step (let them follow)

**Record time:** 5-10 minutes

---

## Video 4: Tell Your Customers (2 min)

**Setup:**
- Talking head + text overlay
- Show email template on screen

**Recording:**
```
"Your site is ready. Let's tell your customers.

Here's an email you can send right now:

[Show email template on screen while reading]

'Hi [Customer],

We just launched a new website: [URL]

You can now:
- Get a quote online
- See our latest work
- Book an appointment

Check it out!'

Send this to existing customers and watch the leads roll in.

Already have an email list? Use it.
Don't have one? Tell customers in person or post on social media."
```

**Tips:**
- Make template visible (screenshot or text overlay)
- Keep it simple (not a production video)

**Record time:** 5 minutes

---

## Video 5: Monthly Checklist (1 min)

**Setup:**
- Talking head + text on screen (checklist)

**Recording:**
```
"Here's what to do each month to keep your site fresh:

Monthly checklist (takes 5 minutes):

1. Update testimonials — add your latest review
2. Check photos — replace blurry/outdated ones
3. Update pricing — if you raised rates
4. Check Google hours — make sure they're correct
5. Review call tracking — are you getting leads?

That's it. One pass per month keeps your site current.

Do this on the 1st of every month. Questions? Email us."
```

**Tips:**
- Show checklist on screen (text overlay or slide)
- Friendly tone (this is routine maintenance)

**Record time:** 3-5 minutes

## Recording Workflow (Batch)

**Total time estimate: 6-8 hours (rough cuts, not perfect)**

**Step 1: Record all 5 videos (2-3 hours)**
- Video 1 (talking head): 5 min
- Video 2 (screen recording): 10 min
- Video 3 (screen recording): 10 min
- Video 4 (talking head): 5 min
- Video 5 (talking head): 5 min
- Retakes: 30 min (things fail, start over)

**Step 2: Quick edits (2-3 hours)**
- Cut dead air (silence trimming)
- Add intro/outro (5 sec each)
- Sync audio levels
- Add captions (auto-generated via YouTube)
- Export MP4 1080p

**Step 3: Upload to YouTube (30 min)**
- Create YouTube channel if needed
- Upload 5 videos as "Unlisted"
- Add captions (auto-generate)
- Get shareable links

**Step 4: Test in email (30 min)**
- Embed video links in test email
- Send to yourself
- Verify playback works
- Check on mobile

**Total: 6-8 hours**

## Tools

**Screen Recording:**
- **Mac:** ScreenFlow ($99, or free trial)
- **Alternative:** QuickTime (built-in, basic)
- **Cross-platform:** OBS (free)

**Editing:**
- **Mac:** iMovie (free, basic)
- **Better:** Adobe Premiere Rush ($10/mo, or free trial)
- **Alternative:** CapCut (free)

**Upload:**
- **Free:** YouTube (best for distribution)
- **Premium:** Vimeo ($75/mo, if privacy preferred)

**Captions:**
- YouTube auto-captions (free, 80% accurate)
- Rev.com ($0.25/min, human captions)
- Kapwing ($10/mo, AI captions)

---

## Quality Standards (Phase 1)

**Acceptable:**
✓ One-take rough videos (authentic)
✓ Built-in mic audio (clear enough)
✓ Natural lighting
✓ Auto-generated captions (80% accuracy okay)
✓ No fancy transitions or effects

**Not required yet:**
✗ Professional voiceover
✗ Complex editing
✗ Multiple takes/retakes
✗ Color grading
✗ Intro/outro animations

**Philosophy:** Rough + real beats polished + fake.

---

## Phase 2 Upgrades

If videos get good feedback:
- Upgrade audio (better mic, Audition editing)
- Add subtitles (human-edited captions)
- Professional intro/outro
- Batch repeat for new customers (make templates)

---

## Success Criteria

✓ All 5 videos recorded
✓ Uploaded to YouTube (unlisted)
✓ Captions working
✓ Embeds in email test
✓ Mobile playback verified
✓ Total time: <8 hours
