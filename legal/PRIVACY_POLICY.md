# BuildFlow Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Introduction

BuildFlow ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly

**Registration & Website Build Information:**
- Business name, owner name, contact email, phone number
- Business address, service areas, hours of operation
- Services you offer and descriptions
- Logo, photos, testimonials, customer reviews
- Pricing information, guarantees, special offers
- Google Business ID, social media handles (optional)

**Payment Information:**
- Name, billing address, card number (processed securely by Stripe)
- We do NOT store full credit card numbers; Stripe handles that

**Support Communications:**
- Emails, support tickets, chat messages you send us
- Feedback, complaints, or suggestions about our service

**Onboarding & Calls:**
- If we hold an onboarding or monthly check-in call with you, we may record it (with your consent)
- Call recordings are kept confidential and used only for quality assurance

### B. Information Collected Automatically

**Server & Hosting Data:**
- IP address of visitors to your site
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited, time spent, navigation patterns
- Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)
- Geographic location (city/country level)
- HTTP referrer (where visitors came from)

**Analytics:**
- Google Analytics data (if you enable it on your site)
- Traffic trends, bounce rates, conversion metrics
- Search keywords that led visitors to your site

**Technical Logs:**
- Server error logs
- SSL/certificate information
- Backup and restore logs
- Site uptime and performance data

## 4. Data Retention

**Your Website Content:**
- Retained as long as your site is active
- Deleted 30 days after you cancel (30-day grace period to export)
- Backups kept for 30 days after cancellation, then deleted

**Server Logs & Analytics:**
- Server logs retained for 90 days, then deleted
- Google Analytics data retained per your Google Analytics settings
- Site visitor IP addresses NOT stored long-term

**Support & Communication Records:**
- Kept for 2 years for dispute resolution
- Deleted after 2 years unless required by law

**Payment Information:**
- Stripe retains payment information per their privacy policy
- We do not retain full credit card numbers

## 5. Data Security

**How We Protect Your Data:**

**Encryption:**
- All site traffic uses HTTPS (SSL certificate)
- Data in transit is encrypted (AES-256)
- Data at rest is encrypted on our servers

**Access Control:**
- BuildFlow staff access is restricted to authorized personnel only
- We use role-based access control (engineers, support, admins have different permissions)
- All access is logged for audit trails

**Backups:**
- Daily automated backups of your site
- Backups are stored on separate secure servers
- Restore testing performed monthly

**Infrastructure:**
- Hosted on Railway (SOC 2 Type II certified cloud provider)
- Cloudflare provides DDoS protection and WAF (Web Application Firewall)
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

**However:**
- No security measure is 100% guaranteed
- We cannot guarantee absolute protection against all attacks
- You are responsible for keeping your credentials secure

## 7. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

**If You Are In Europe (GDPR):**
- Right to access: request a copy of your personal data
- Right to correct: request corrections to inaccurate data
- Right to delete: request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing: limit how we use your data
- Right to data portability: receive your data in a portable format
- Right to object: object to processing for marketing purposes
- Right to withdraw consent: withdraw consent anytime (with notice)

**If You Are In California (CCPA):**
- Right to know: request what personal information we collect
- Right to delete: request deletion of personal information
- Right to opt-out: opt out of "sale" of personal information (we don't sell it)
- Right to non-discrimination: we don't discriminate for exercising these rights
- Right to correct: request correction of inaccurate data

**To Exercise These Rights:**
- Email: privacy@buildflow.com
- Subject line: "Data Request" + your name
- We will respond within 30 days
- No fee required

## 8. Cookies & Tracking

**Cookies We Use:**
- **Session cookies:** keep you logged in during support sessions
- **Preference cookies:** remember your settings
- **Analytics cookies:** track site performance (Google Analytics)

**Cookies We Don't Use:**
