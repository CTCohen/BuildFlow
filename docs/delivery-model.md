# Delivery & ownership model — BuildFlow

## Core principle

**One multi-tenant codebase. BuildFlow hosts and maintains every managed site. No code,
hosting, or DNS is ever handed to a managed client.** Each site is the same Astro app
rendering a per-client content folder (`content/<slug>/`) + `theme.json`. A bug fixed once
is fixed everywhere. There is no per-client code.

## Packages

### B — Managed Growth  ·  $99/mo  (DEFAULT / lead offer)

Included:
- Hosting, SSL, CDN, custom domain
- Local SEO + AI SEO
- Unlimited small content edits (client emails them in)
- Monthly check-in
- Lead-capture machinery: fast site, click-to-call, quote/contact forms routed to owner

Terms:
- Annual option: $990/yr (2 months free)
- Optional $199 setup fee — waived for launch / first clients
- Cancel → site goes offline. Static export available as a one-time service (see A pricing).
- **We promise the machinery, not lead volume.** Copy: "built to capture leads," never
  "you will get N leads."

### A — Ownership  ·  $497 one-time  (DOWNSELL — offer only when they balk at monthly)

- Static starter site, exported and deployed to the **client's own** host/account
- 1 month of transition support, then no ongoing relationship
- Client owns the files
- **Hosting, domain, and email bills become the client's responsibility at handoff** —
  stated in writing in the transition doc
- $497 sits at the top of the going rate for cold-outreach AI-built sites ($400–500) and
  covers the ~1–2 hrs of export/handoff work
- "You host it" is what keeps B the easier choice — no big price gap needed

_First 3–10 clients: lead **B-only**, prove the model, keep A in the back pocket._

## Go-to-market rule

**Cold outreach leads with B.** A is only surfaced when a prospect rejects a monthly fee.
Never promise lead *volume* in B — promise the lead-capture machinery.

## How this landed (market research, 2026-09-07)

- The cold-outreach / AI-built niche charges ~$500 one-time **plus** a $97–197/mo retainer;
  pure one-time-forever sellers churn and burn out.
- WaaS for local business (ABDigital, Kosmorph) is an established $0-build / $99/mo model —
  this is package B.
- Freelance/agency trades sites run $1,500–4,500 — won't convert on cold outreach, so a
  higher A price was wrong for this channel.
- Original $375/$99 instinct was close; changes: A → $497, "you host it" framing, 1-mo
  support, B is the default not a coequal option.

## Open pricing questions

- Tiering B later: $99 / $149 / $249 once SEO effort per client is known
- Hybrid once SEO time is known: $497 build + $99/mo
- Setup-fee-on-B as a seriousness filter vs. friction
