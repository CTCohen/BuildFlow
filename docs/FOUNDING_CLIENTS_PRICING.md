# Founding Clients Pricing — DRAFT, not yet approved

Ties directly to D02 (price-increase timing, provisional) and D03 (soft-launch cohort, provisional) in
`DECISIONS.md`. This does not go live, does not get quoted to anyone, until Tyler approves it.

## Proposed pricing (locked for life of the customer, not just an intro rate)
| Tier | Standard | Founding | Discount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | $149/mo | $119/mo | ~20% off |
| SMB | $249/mo | $199/mo | ~20% off |
| Mid-Market | paused, not offered | — | — |

Annual stays 10x the founding monthly rate (same ratio as standard), so Micro founding annual = $1,190,
SMB founding annual = $1,990.

**Why locked for life, not just an intro period:** a temporary discount that jumps to full price at renewal reads
as a bait-and-switch to a first customer who took a real risk on an unproven company. Locking it permanently is
the actual reward — it also caps your downside, since founding cohort size should be small (recommend capping at
the same 5-prospect soft-launch group per D03, or up to 10-15 if you want more room).

## Founding client benefits ("you have my ear" package)
- **Direct line to Tyler** — a real phone number or a promise of same-day email response, not the standard 24-48h
  support SLA (D37)
- **First look at new features** — founding customers get anything new before it's announced generally
- **No price increase, ever, for this account** — while everyone else's pricing may rise (D02), founding
  customers are explicitly exempt
- **Input on the roadmap** — a standing invite to say what they'd want built next; doesn't mean it gets built,
  but they get heard first
- **A public "founding customer" badge/mention** (only if they want it — opt-in, never assumed) — useful social
  proof for them too if they're comfortable being named

## What this is NOT
- Not a discount code anyone can use — cohort-limited, tied to specific real accounts
- Not the same as the Offboard tier's one-time pricing — this only applies to Managed
- Not free — still a paying customer, just at a locked-in rate

## Recommended trigger
Offer this only to the initial soft-launch cohort (the 5 Phoenix HVAC prospects, or however many real customers
close before the first price increase). Once standard pricing moves (D02), founding pricing closes — no new
founding customers after that point, or the discount stops meaning anything.
