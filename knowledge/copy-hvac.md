# HVAC Copy Bank — Real Pain Points

## Rule
HVAC buyers are in two states: (1) AC died today, panicking, (2) system old, shopping calmly. Copy addresses the emergency mindset first, then the long-term value. No "world-class HVAC solutions."

## Why
HVAC is a grudge purchase. People don't want to talk about it; they want it fixed fast and fairly. Copy that acknowledges this ("AC died? We get it.") builds instant trust vs. generic corporate tone.

## Headlines — Emergency (Broke)
- "AC stopped? We're here in 2 hours"
- "AC gone out? Same-day repair, not next week"
- "[City] AC repair — today, not tomorrow"
- "Your AC is broken. We'll fix it fast."

## Headlines — Planning (Shopping)
- "Save $3,000+ replacing your AC smarter"
- "Phoenix AC repair: 10 years, 2,000+ homes served"
- "Heat pump upgrade saves [average $/year] in cooling costs"

## Taglines
- "Same-day service, fair price, real guarantee"
- "AC repair when it matters. Fast, honest, local."
- "[Years] years keeping [city] cool"

## Trust Signals to Lead With
- Years in business (e.g., "Since 2014")
- License # prominently (e.g., "AZ-HVAC-88402")
- Specific certifications (EPA 608, NATE-certified techs)
- Service speed (e.g., "Usually within 4 hours")
- Guarantee length (e.g., "1-year warranty on all work")
- Team size if small (e.g., "8-person team, all EPA-certified")

## Real Service Copy (vs. Placeholder)
❌ "We handle AC repair."
✓ "Compressor dead, refrigerant leak, or capacitor failed? We diagnose and fix it the same day."

❌ "Installation available."
✓ "New system sizing, install, and rebate paperwork handled start to finish."

❌ "Maintenance plans offered."
✓ "Spring tune-up: $99. Catches problems before they fail and keeps your warranty valid."

## Objection Handling in Copy
**"Will they overcharge me?"**
→ "Upfront pricing before work starts. No surprises."

**"How long until someone comes?"**
→ "Most calls before 2pm get same-day service."

**"Am I locked into a contract?"**
→ "No service plan required. One-time repair, annual tune-up, or maintenance plan — you choose."

## Check
- [ ] No generic "world-class" language
- [ ] Emergency path and planning path both present
- [ ] License # and years in business visible on homepage
- [ ] At least one guarantee (warranty length) stated
- [ ] Service copy includes specific problem types, not just service names
- [ ] CTA matches mindset (emergency sites: "Call now"; planning sites: "Get quote")
