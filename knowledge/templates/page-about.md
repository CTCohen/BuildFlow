# About page (`/about`)

Structure:
1. **H1** — "About `<Business>`".
2. Story paragraph — first person plural: founded when, by whom, why, service area, the
   one thing they do differently. Real, specific, no mission-statement filler.
3. **Proof row** — years, jobs completed, response window, licence #, insurance.
4. **Team** — real photos + first names + role if available. Skip if no real photos
   (never stock people).
5. **Credentials** — licence, NATE/EPA, BBB, manufacturer dealer — only what they hold.
6. Reviews block.
7. CTA.

## Check
- QA: `/about` has an H1, a story section > 60 words, a proof row with ≥ 3 numeric/verified
  facts, and no stock-photo people (reviewer spot-check).
- Copy: passes the anti-ai-slop grep.
