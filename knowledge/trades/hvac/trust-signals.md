# HVAC trust signals

Show only the ones the business actually holds (verify against their site / GBP).

- **Licence:** Arizona ROC number, displayed as "Licensed ROC #`<n>` · Bonded & Insured".
- **Certifications:** NATE-certified technicians, EPA Section 608, BBB accreditation +
  rating.
- **Manufacturer status:** Trane Comfort Specialist / Carrier Factory Authorized /
  Lennox Premier / Goodman / Rheem Pro Partner — whichever applies.
- **Tenure & volume:** "`<n>` years in `<metro>`", "`<n>`+ installs", "`<n>` five-star reviews".
- **Guarantees:** workmanship warranty length, "100% satisfaction / we make it right",
  "upfront pricing — the price we quote is the price you pay".
- **People:** real tech photos, first names, "you talk to a technician, not a call center".
- **Local:** named service-area suburbs, family-owned if true, community ties if real.

Placement: a condensed trust strip under the hero; full detail on `/about`; badges near
each CTA.

## Check
- QA: hero trust strip present with licence + insured + years.
- Reviewer: every badge/claim shown is verifiable against the business's own site or GBP —
  no invented certifications.
