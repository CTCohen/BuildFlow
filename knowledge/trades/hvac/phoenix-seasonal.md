# Phoenix HVAC seasonal context

Phoenix metro: extreme cooling demand, mild heating season, monsoon dust. Cooling is
life-safety May–October (regular 110–118°F).

## Build-time (bake into copy)
- Lead with AC / cooling, not heating. Heating is secondary.
- Emphasize **same-day / emergency AC** — a dead AC in July is an emergency.
- Call out **dust + monsoon** impact: clogged filters, capacitor failures, coil fouling.
- Mention **energy bills** (long cooling season = high bills → efficiency upsell) and
  **SRP / APS rebates** + federal high-efficiency / heat-pump tax credits.
- Maintenance framed as "pre-summer AC tune-up" (Feb–April) and "post-monsoon check".

## Managed-phase (seasonal GBP posts + content refresh)
| Month | Angle |
|---|---|
| Feb–Apr | "Beat the summer rush — book your AC tune-up now" |
| May–Sep | "Same-day AC repair", heat-safety, "is it worth repairing or replacing?" |
| Jul–Sep | monsoon: surge protection, capacitor/contactor failures, filter changes |
| Oct–Nov | "Post-summer system check", heat-pump / furnace readiness |
| Dec–Jan | mild-heat tips, thermostat, indoor air quality |
