# Roofing buyer objections

## Rule
| Objection | Where it is answered | How |
|---|---|---|
| Are they licensed and insured? | hero trust strip, about | license number and insurance line as supplied |
| Repair or replace? | repair and replacement pages | explain how the decision is made |
| Will they be honest about the damage? | service pages | photo-backed findings |
| What will it cost? | service pages | explain how written estimates work if offered |
| Will they handle the insurance paperwork? | storm-damage page | state only what the business actually does |
| Are they local? | area pages | named areas and real reviews |

If a page asks for a call, every objection here must be answerable from what is visible on that page or one click away.

## Why
Each unanswered worry is a lost call. Putting the answer where the worry arises removes the reason to leave.

## Check
- Reviewer walkthrough: on the home page and one service page, every objection has a visible or one-click answer.
