# Roofing keyword to page map

## Rule
Each page targets one primary intent. `<city>` is the page's city.

| Page | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | roofing contractor <metro> | roofer <metro> |
| `/services/roof-repair` | roof repair <city> | roof repair near me, tile roof repair |
| `/services/roof-replacement` | roof replacement <city> | new roof <city> |
| `/services/leak-repair` | roof leak repair <city> | leaking roof <city> |
| `/services/inspection` | roof inspection <city> | roof inspection before buying a home |
| `/services/storm-damage` | storm damage roof repair <city> | monsoon roof damage |
| `/services/maintenance` | roof maintenance <city> | roof cleaning and checkup |

The primary keyword appears once, naturally, in the title, H1, URL, and first paragraph. Do not create a page for something the business does not do.

## Why
One intent per page keeps pages from competing and matches how customers describe the problem.

## Check
- SEO audit: each page's title and H1 contain its primary keyword; no two pages share a primary.
