# Roofing trust signals

## Rule
Show only what the business actually holds, verified against their own site or listings:
- State contractor license number
- Bonded and insured, if true
- Manufacturer certifications, only if held
- Workmanship warranty length, as stated by the business
- Years in business and roofs completed, only with real numbers
- Real project photos with permission
- Real technician photos and first names

Placement: a short trust strip under the hero, detail on `/about`, and a badge near each call to action.

## Why
Trust signals answer "can I rely on you" before the visitor has to ask. An invented one destroys trust and can create legal exposure.

## Check
- QA: the hero trust strip shows only supplied facts.
- Reviewer: every badge is verifiable against the business's own materials.
