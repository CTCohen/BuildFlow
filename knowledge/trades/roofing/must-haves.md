# Roofing website must-haves

## Rule
Above the fold on mobile, in this order: business name, what they do and where ("Roofing contractor in <city>"), a tap-to-call button, availability (only if the business confirms it), and rating with review count if real. A sticky tap-to-call bar stays on scroll.

Must appear somewhere on the site:
- License number (state contractor license) and "licensed, bonded and insured", if held
- Years in business and a named service-area list
- Service pages: repair, replacement, leak repair, inspection, storm damage, maintenance
- Photo-based estimates explained (real before and after photos only)
- Material options explained plainly: shingle, tile, foam, metal
- Written estimate and warranty language, only as the business states it
- Embedded real reviews plus short quotes
- A short quote form

## Why
Roofing buyers are usually deciding fast, often under stress. They check: is this business local, licensed, able to come when I need them, and honest about cost. A page that answers those four questions quickly converts.

## Check
- QA: the home page above the fold has a `tel:` link, the city, and the trade.
- QA: every item above that the business supplied appears; no item appears that they did not supply.
- Reviewer: nothing shown (license, badge, hours) is unverified.
