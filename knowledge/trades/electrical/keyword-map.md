# Electrical keyword to page map

## Rule
Each page targets one primary intent. `<city>` is the page's city.

| Page | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | electrician <metro> | electrical contractor <metro> |
| `/services/panel-upgrade` | panel upgrade <city> | electrical panel replacement <city> |
| `/services/wiring-outlets` | outlet repair <city> | electrician outlets <city>, rewiring |
| `/services/lighting` | lighting installation <city> | recessed lighting <city>, ceiling fan install |
| `/services/ev-charger` | EV charger installation <city> | home EV charger installer <city> |
| `/services/troubleshooting` | electrical repair <city> | breaker keeps tripping <city> |
| `/services/emergency-electrical` | emergency electrician <city> | 24 hour electrician <city> |

The primary keyword appears once, naturally, in the title, H1, URL, and first paragraph. Do not create a page for something the business does not do.

## Why
One intent per page keeps pages from competing and matches how customers describe the problem.

## Check
- SEO audit: each page's title and H1 contain its primary keyword; no two pages share a primary.
