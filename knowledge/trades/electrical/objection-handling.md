# Electrical buyer objections

## Rule
| Objection | Where it is answered | How |
|---|---|---|
| Are they licensed? | hero trust strip, about | license number as supplied |
| Is the work safe and permitted? | service pages | explain permits and inspections where they apply |
| Can they come now? | hero | only if the business confirms availability |
| Will it cost more than I expect? | service pages | explain how quotes work if the business offers them |
| Do they do my type of job? | service pages | one page per service |
| Are they local? | area pages | named areas and real reviews |

If a page asks for a call, every objection here must be answerable from what is visible on that page or one click away.

## Why
Each unanswered worry is a lost call. Putting the answer where the worry arises removes the reason to leave.

## Check
- Reviewer walkthrough: on the home page and one service page, every objection has a visible or one-click answer.
