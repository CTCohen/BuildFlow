# Electrical website must-haves

## Rule
Above the fold on mobile, in this order: business name, what they do and where ("Electrician in <city>"), a tap-to-call button, availability (only if the business confirms it), and rating with review count if real. A sticky tap-to-call bar stays on scroll.

Must appear somewhere on the site:
- License number (state contractor license) and "licensed, bonded and insured", if held
- Years in business and a named service-area list
- Service pages: panel upgrades, outlets and wiring, lighting, EV chargers, troubleshooting
- Emergency availability, stated only if offered
- A safety note on burning smells and sparks (builds trust)
- Permit language: a licensed electrician handles permits where required
- Embedded real reviews plus short quotes
- A short quote form

## Why
Electrical buyers are usually deciding fast, often under stress. They check: is this business local, licensed, able to come when I need them, and honest about cost. A page that answers those four questions quickly converts.

## Check
- QA: the home page above the fold has a `tel:` link, the city, and the trade.
- QA: every item above that the business supplied appears; no item appears that they did not supply.
- Reviewer: nothing shown (license, badge, hours) is unverified.
