# Plumbing keyword to page map

## Rule
Each page targets one primary intent. `<city>` is the page's city.

| Page | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | plumber <metro> | plumbing repair <metro> |
| `/services/drain-cleaning` | drain cleaning <city> | clogged drain <city>, slow drain, sewer backup |
| `/services/water-heater` | water heater repair <city> | water heater replacement <city>, no hot water <city> |
| `/services/leak-repair` | leak repair <city> | water leak detection <city>, slab leak |
| `/services/repiping` | repiping <city> | whole house repipe <city> |
| `/services/sewer-line` | sewer line repair <city> | sewer camera inspection <city> |
| `/services/emergency-plumbing` | emergency plumber <city> | 24 hour plumber <city> |

The primary keyword appears once, naturally, in the title, H1, URL, and first paragraph. Do not create a page for something the business does not do.

## Why
One intent per page keeps pages from competing and matches how customers describe the problem.

## Check
- SEO audit: each page's title and H1 contain its primary keyword; no two pages share a primary.
