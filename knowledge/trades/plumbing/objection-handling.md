# Plumbing buyer objections

## Rule
| Objection | Where it is answered | How |
|---|---|---|
| Can they come right now? | hero and every service opener | state availability only if the business confirms it |
| Are they licensed and insured? | hero trust strip, about, footer | license number and insurance line as supplied |
| Will they overcharge me? | service pages | explain how quotes work if the business offers upfront quotes |
| Will they tear up my house? | leak and sewer pages | explain inspection methods that limit damage |
| Do they know my problem? | service pages | symptom-first descriptions |
| Are they local? | area pages | named areas, real local reviews |

If a page asks for a call, every objection here must be answerable from what is visible on that page or one click away.

## Why
Each unanswered worry is a lost call. Putting the answer where the worry arises removes the reason to leave.

## Check
- Reviewer walkthrough: on the home page and one service page, every objection has a visible or one-click answer.
