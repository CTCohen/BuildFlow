# Plumbing Phoenix-area context

## Rule
- Hard water is common in much of the Phoenix area and can shorten the life of water heaters and fixtures.
- Summer heat stresses water heaters and outdoor plumbing.
- Many homes are built on concrete slabs, so leaks can occur under the foundation.
- Monsoon storms can cause drainage and sewer problems.

These are general observations for copy, not verified facts. Each must be confirmed before it is used on a client site (`knowledge/pool/cities/phoenix.json` marks them unverified).

## Why
Local context makes copy specific to the area, which builds trust and helps local search.
