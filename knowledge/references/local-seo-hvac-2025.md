# HVAC local SEO ranking factors — 2025 web roundup

Synthesised from multiple 2025–2026 HVAC-marketing guides (InvoiceFly, Elevarus,
MarketingPracticality, FieldServicely, Freitag).

## Takeaways
1. Local Pack ranking = **relevance + distance + prominence**. Google Business Profile
   signals ≈ 32% of the decision; review signals ≈ 20% (up from 16% in 2023). Together
   >half the map-pack outcome.
2. On-page signals ≈ 15%. Location keywords in title, meta, H1, and dedicated
   service-in-city pages.
3. **Review volume + recency beats rating perfection.** 80 reviews @ 4.8★ outranks and
   out-converts 15 @ 5.0★. Steady new reviews year-round matters more than a burst.
4. GBP must list heating and cooling as **separate services**, carry regular photos, and
   post weekly Google Posts that change with the season.
5. Service-type × city pages ("AC repair in Chandler", "furnace install in Mesa") are
   the on-site structure that ranks — not one generic "HVAC Services" page.
6. NAP must be identical across the site, GBP, and citations.

## Adopt
- Site ships: LocalBusiness/HVACBusiness schema, NAP in footer + contact page matching
  GBP exactly, a service hub linking service×area pages, embedded Google reviews.
- `hvac/page-map` includes service×area page generation.
- Managed monthly SOP: new-review prompt cadence + weekly seasonal GBP post.

## Test
- QA: schema valid; NAP string identical in footer/contact/schema.
- SEO audit: each site has ≥ 4 service pages and ≥ 1 service-area page; city in title/H1.

## Reject / defer
- Nothing rejected. Citation-building (Yelp/BBB/directory listings) deferred to managed phase.
