# Generative Engine Optimization — 2025 web roundup

Synthesised from 2025–2026 GEO playbooks (Frase, Enrich Labs, Passionfruit, SEOtuners,
LLMrefs).

## Takeaways
1. GEO = being *in the answer* on ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI Overviews / Claude,
   not just ranking a blue link.
2. Structure content with a **direct answer in the first 40–60 words** of a section.
3. **Fact density**: a concrete number/spec every ~150–200 words.
4. Cite authoritative sources; use proper schema (FAQ, HowTo, LocalBusiness).
5. Platform tilt: ChatGPT favours encyclopedic/definitional content; Perplexity rewards
   recency + real examples; AI Overviews lean on content already ranking well in classic SEO.
6. AI-referred sessions grew sharply in 2025 and reportedly convert far above organic —
   low competition now = cheap citation share.
7. `llms.txt` at site root: a plain-text map of the site's key pages for LLM crawlers.

## Adopt
- Every generated site: `/llms.txt`, FAQ schema on the home + service pages, each section
  opens with a one-sentence direct answer, service pages state real specs/numbers
  (response window, price ranges, warranty length, brands).
- Entity consistency: business name, service names, and area names phrased identically everywhere.

## Test
- `searchfit-seo:ai-visibility` check passes: llms.txt present, FAQ/HowTo schema valid,
  sections lead with a direct answer, entities consistent.

## Reject / defer
- Off-site GEO (getting cited on Reddit/industry sites) deferred to managed phase.
