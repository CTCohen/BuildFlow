# Plumbing Copy Bank — Real Pain Points

## Rule
Plumbing buyers panic about two things: (1) water damage RIGHT NOW (burst pipe, backed-up sewage), (2) money wasted (hidden leaks costing $100/month). Copy addresses "I need help immediately" and "I need to understand what's really wrong."

## Why
Plumbing emergencies are time-critical and costly. People need reassurance that the plumber won't tear up the house to fix a $50 problem. Copy that shows diagnostic depth ("we find before we fix") builds trust vs. "we do plumbing."

## Headlines — Emergency (Burst/Backup)
- "Burst pipe? We stop the damage and fix it today"
- "Backed-up drain? Same-day diagnosis and clearing"
- "Sewer smell? We find the problem without digging"
- "[City] emergency plumbing — we answer 24/7"

## Headlines — Planning (Leak/Maintenance)
- "Save $1,200/year: find your hidden water leak"
- "Plumbing keeps breaking? We redesign it right"
- "[City] plumbing: licensed, honest, no upsell"

## Taglines
- "Fast diagnosis, honest repair, no surprises"
- "Leak found without tearing up your house"
- "[Years] years fixing [city] pipes"

## Trust Signals to Lead With
- Years in business (e.g., "Since 2017")
- License # prominently (e.g., "AZ-ROC-235789")
- Specific tools/methods (e.g., "Video inspection finds problems without digging")
- Emergency availability (e.g., "24/7 emergency line")
- Specific cost savings (e.g., "Hidden leak repair: $200–$600, saves ~$1,200/year in water waste")
- Honesty signal (e.g., "We tell you what needs fixing and what doesn't")

## Real Service Copy (vs. Placeholder)
❌ "Drain cleaning services available."
✓ "Clogged drain? We use video inspection to see the exact problem (debris, tree roots, collapsed line) and clear it without tearing up your yard."

❌ "We detect leaks."
✓ "Suspicious water bill? We find leaks in water lines, under slabs, and behind walls without excavation. Video proof included."

❌ "Water heater repair/replacement."
✓ "Tank or tankless: we size, install, and handle rebate paperwork. One-year warranty on all parts and labor."

❌ "Plumbing repair."
✓ "Burst pipe, split line, or frozen? We stop the damage immediately, assess the full picture, and recommend repair or replacement."

## Objection Handling in Copy
**"Will they dig up my yard?"**
→ "We use video inspection first. Most leaks fixed without excavation."

**"How much will this cost?"**
→ "Upfront estimate before work starts. No surprises."

**"What if it's not actually broken?"**
→ "We tell you honestly. If it's not worth fixing yet, we'll say so."

**"Is 24/7 emergency really available?"**
→ "Yes. Call anytime; we answer the emergency line. Surcharge applies for off-hours, but it's transparent upfront."

## Check
- [ ] No generic "professional plumbing" language
- [ ] Emergency path and planning path both present
- [ ] License # and years in business visible on homepage
- [ ] Specific diagnostic tool mentioned (video inspection, leak detection method)
- [ ] At least one cost-saving stat (e.g., "saves $X/year")
- [ ] Service copy includes specific problem types (burst, clog, leak) not just service names
- [ ] Honesty/no-upsell signal present
- [ ] CTA is urgent for emergency path, solution-focused for planning path
