## Vertical Selection Criteria

### High-Priority Verticals (Recurring Revenue + Emergency Calls)
- ✅ HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical
- Pattern: Emergency calls = immediate lead capture
- Website benefit: Highly valuable to prospects
- Revenue: Monthly subscription strong fit

### Medium-Priority Verticals (Seasonal + Project-Based)
- ✅ Roofing, Tree Care, Snow Removal
- Pattern: Seasonal spike = concentrated lead capture
- Website benefit: Moderate value (competitors online)
- Revenue: Managed workable, Offboard (one-time) also attractive

### Lower-Priority Verticals (High Volume, Low Price, High Churn)
- ⚠️ Cleaning, Pest Control, Pressure Washing
- Pattern: Volume-based, low cost per job
- Website benefit: Nice-to-have, not critical
- Revenue: Managed risky (price sensitivity), Offboard may fit better

### Specialized Verticals (Low Volume, High Value)
- ⚠️ Security, Environmental Testing, Disaster Restoration
- Pattern: Long sales cycles, high-value jobs
- Website benefit: Professional credibility critical
- Revenue: Subscription less common; project-based normal

## Vertical-Specific Considerations

### HVAC
- **Pattern:** Emergency calls year-round, peak summer/winter
- **Website value:** High (captures emergency leads, builds trust)
- **Revenue model:** Managed subscription ($149 Micro / $249 SMB per month) is a strong fit, recurring calls
- **Pricing:** Mid-range ($50–150 per service call)
- **SEO keywords:** "AC repair near me", "emergency HVAC", "furnace service"
- **Trust signals:** Licensing, emergency response time, reviews

### Plumbing
- **Pattern:** Emergency calls year-round, similar to HVAC
- **Website value:** Very high (emergency lead capture critical)
- **Revenue model:** Subscription strong, recurring service volume
- **Pricing:** Mid-range ($75–200 per service call)
- **SEO keywords:** "emergency plumber", "pipe repair", "drain cleaning"
- **Trust signals:** Licensing, 24/7 availability, warranty on work

### Electrical
- **Pattern:** Mixed (emergency + project work like panel upgrades)
- **Website value:** High (builds credibility for complex jobs)
- **Revenue model:** Subscription, but fewer recurring calls
- **Pricing:** Higher ($100–250+ per hour)
- **SEO keywords:** "electrician near me", "electrical panel", "EV charger install"
- **Trust signals:** Master license, insurance, code compliance

### Roofing
- **Pattern:** Project-based, seasonal (spring/fall inspections, winter damage)
- **Website value:** High (inspires confidence for large projects)
- **Revenue model:** Subscription workable, but one-time Offboard also fits
- **Pricing:** High ($5k–50k per project)
- **SEO keywords:** "roof repair", "new roof cost", "roof replacement"
- **Trust signals:** Warranty on materials/labor, before/after photos, insurance

### Landscaping
- **Pattern:** Seasonal (spring through fall), project + recurring maintenance
- **Website value:** Moderate (many competitors online, hard to differentiate)
- **Revenue model:** Subscription workable (monthly maintenance contracts)
- **Pricing:** Low-to-mid ($200–2k per project, $100–300/month recurring)
- **SEO keywords:** "landscaping near me", "lawn maintenance", "garden design"
- **Trust signals:** Portfolio photos, maintenance guarantees, equipment photos

### Cleaning
- **Pattern:** Recurring (janitorial, residential monthly)
- **Website value:** Low-to-moderate (commoditized market)
- **Revenue model:** Subscription fits (cleaning contracts), but price-sensitive
- **Pricing:** Low ($150–500/month for residential)
- **SEO keywords:** "house cleaning near me", "commercial cleaning", "office cleaning"
- **Trust signals:** Eco-friendly products, staff background checks, insurance

## Competitive Landscape

### Verticals with Strong Online Presence (Harder to win)
- Roofing (major national chains + local pros with sites)
- Landscaping (many pros have basic sites)
- Plumbing (some established players online)

### Verticals with Weak Online Presence (Easier win)
- HVAC (many pros still rely on word-of-mouth)
- Electrical (fewer established players online)
- Tree Care (very few have professional sites)
- Pool Maintenance (mostly outdated/no sites)
