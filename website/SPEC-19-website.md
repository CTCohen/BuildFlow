## System 19: Fornax Public Marketing Website

**Domain:** [domain TBD under Fornax name]. **Audience:** prospective trades SMB customers. **Architecture:** separate from [domain TBD under Fornax name] (Tyler's CRM) and customer dashboards.

---

## 1. Site Structure

Home (value prop + CTA), Features (product capabilities), Pricing (transparent tiers + FAQ), Case Studies (3-5 proof points), Demo ("see your site in 60 seconds"), Blog (SEO + education), Docs (public, no login), About (Tyler's story).

---

## 2. Home Page Design

**Hero:** "Your AI-Powered Website Builder for Trades" / "Generate qualified leads automatically in under 60 seconds" — CTAs: See Demo (primary), Start Free Trial (secondary)

**Trust section:** logo grid of early customers

**Three-column features:** AI-Designed Websites, Real Leads in Real Time, Easy CRM Sync

**Social proof:** testimonials + video

**FAQ (SEO-friendly):** timeline, customization, existing-site migration, technical skill needed, cancellation

---

## 3. Features Page

Lead Generation Engine (AI scoring, real-time push, CRM auto-create), AI-Designed Websites (brand preservation, 8-10 styling profiles, one-click customization, redesign service), CRM Integrations (top 10 trades CRMs, feedback loop), Analytics & Reporting (dashboard, monthly report, ROI calculator), Customer Support (email, help center)

---

## 4. Pricing Page

Micro $149/mo (50 leads, 1 site), SMB $249/mo (500 leads, 3 sites), Market Leader $399/mo (unlimited leads, 10 sites) — each with 14-day free trial. Add-ons: Professional Redesign ($199-499), Done-For-You Setup ($99), Annual discount (2 months free)

---

## 5. Case Studies Page

Structure per case: company/industry, before (problem), after (solution+results with metrics), quote, before/after screenshot if approved

---

## 6. Demo Page

Flow: email/phone → "build my site in 60 sec" → quick form → demo dashboard preview (sample site + live leads animation + CRM status) → "start free trial" CTA

---

## 7. Blog & Educational Content

Topics: lead generation, website design tips, why ads fail for service companies, traffic vs. conversion. Cadence: 1-2 posts/week Phase 1 (manual). CTA at end of every post.

---

## 8. Docs (Public, No Login)

Getting Started, Integrations (per-CRM setup), Troubleshooting, Best Practices — SEO-indexed, no login wall.

---

## 9. About Page

Tyler's founder story, mission (give small contractors Fortune-500-grade lead gen), commitment (no hidden fees, cancel anytime, real support).

---

## 10. UX & Design Principles

Mobile-first (majority traffic), Lighthouse 80+ / <2s load, 1-2 clear CTAs per page, trust signals (testimonials, security badges), minimal friction (3-field forms max)

---

## 11. Content & Messaging Framework

**Core message:** "Qualified leads, automatically. Your website does the work."
**Three pillars:** Speed, Quality, Ease
**Tone:** casual but professional, direct/practical, action-oriented

---

## 12. Roadmap

Phase 1: Home/Features/Pricing/1 Case Study/Demo/Blog(3 posts)/basic Docs, mobile-responsive, Lighthouse 80+. Phase 2: +2 case studies, expanded blog, video testimonials, Calendly. Phase 3: customer stories, comparison page, integration directory, video tutorials.

**Content Agent note:** blog is manually written in Phase 1 (per Agent Registry — no agent built yet); a Content Agent is a reasonable Phase 2 addition once posting volume justifies it.
