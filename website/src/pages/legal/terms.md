## 1. Overview

BuildFlow ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") provides automated website design, hosting, and maintenance services to service-based businesses ("you," "your," or "Customer"). By purchasing a BuildFlow plan, you agree to these Terms of Service.

**Service:** BuildFlow builds a website for your business based on information you provide, then hosts and maintains it on our infrastructure. Under the Ownership plan, we export a static version of the site to your own host and end the ongoing relationship.

**Plans & Pricing:**

- **Managed Growth — $99/mo (default):** Hosting, SSL, CDN, custom domain, local + AI SEO, unlimited small content edits, monthly check-in, and lead-capture machinery (fast site, click-to-call, quote/contact forms routed to you). After that, there is no ongoing BuildFlow service. You own the exported files. Hosting, domain, and email costs become your responsibility at handoff.

Cold outreach is quoted at Managed Growth. Ownership is offered only if you decline a monthly plan.
